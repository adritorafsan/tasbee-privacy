Privacy policy

Effective October 23, 2026

Tasbee is a dhikr counter made by Adrito Rafsan. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no server of its own. Everything you do in the app stays on your phone. This policy explains exactly what that means.

What Tasbee keeps on your phone

To work, Tasbee saves the following in its own private storage on your device. Nobody else can read it — not us, and not other apps.

  • The name you choose to be greeted by, if you enter one.
  • Your daily goal, your dhikr and their targets, any custom dhikr you write, and your counts.
  • Your streak and streak shields.
  • Which apps you chose to pause, and how many skips you have used today.
  • How many reminders a day you want.
  • Whether you have bought Premium, so the app knows what to unlock.

Pausing other apps (Android)

If you choose to turn on app pausing, Android asks you for two permissions, and Tasbee explains both before you are sent to grant them.

Usage access lets Tasbee see the name of the app that is currently open, so it can tell when one of the apps you chose has been opened. That is the only thing it reads. Tasbee never reads what is inside another app — no messages, no screens, no typing, no passwords — and it does not keep a history of the apps you use.

Display over other apps lets Tasbee show its dhikr pause in front of the app you opened.

While pausing is on, Android shows an ongoing notification saying so. You can turn pausing off in Tasbee’s Settings, or take either permission away in your phone’s settings, at any time. The rest of Tasbee keeps working without them.

The list of apps on your phone

When you choose apps to pause, Tasbee shows the apps on your phone that have a home-screen icon, so you can pick from them. This list is read on your phone, used only for that screen, and never saved or sent anywhere. Tasbee only remembers the apps you picked.

Reminders and widgets

Daily reminders are scheduled on your phone by the app itself. No push service or server is involved, and you can turn notifications off in your phone’s settings.

Home-screen widgets show your progress using a copy of your counts that stays on your phone.

Buying Premium

Premium is a one-time purchase made through Google Play (or the App Store on iPhone). The store handles the payment, and your card or payment details are never shared with Tasbee or with us. The store’s own privacy policy covers that payment.

To unlock Premium, Tasbee asks the store whether this account owns it. The store may also give us basic order details — such as an order number, the product, the price and the country — so we can handle refunds and support.

What we do not do

  • We do not have accounts, and we do not ask for your email, phone number or location.
  • We do not use ads, analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone.
  • We do not back your data up to the cloud. Android backup is switched off for Tasbee, so your data does not follow you to a new phone.

Deleting your data

Because your data only lives on your phone, you are in control of it. Uninstalling Tasbee, or clearing its storage in your phone’s settings, deletes all of it permanently. A Premium purchase stays with your store account and can be restored from Settings → Restore purchases.

Children

Tasbee is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If Tasbee ever starts handling information differently, this policy will be updated before that change ships, with a new effective date.
